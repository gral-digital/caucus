# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**In short**: be respectful, assume good faith, no harassment or personal
attacks, keep discussions technical. Disagreements are resolved with
arguments and measurements, not volume.

Reports: see the contact in [.github/SECURITY.md](.github/SECURITY.md)
(same channel, mark the subject as "Code of Conduct"). Reports are handled
confidentially by the maintainers.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant ladder: correction → warning →
temporary ban → permanent ban.
